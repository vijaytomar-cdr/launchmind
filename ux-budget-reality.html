Executive summary
AllignX faces a classic marketplace chicken-and-egg problem compounded by a Day 1 UX drop-off that kills conversion before users ever experience the product's core value. The previous Meta and Google spend likely failed because: (1) creative was feature-first not pain-first, (2) provider supply was too thin… The 90-day strategy prioritises supply-side density first (30 days), then demand activation with social proof creative (60 days), then scaled referral and paid growth (90 days).
